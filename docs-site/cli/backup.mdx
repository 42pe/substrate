---
title: "substrate backup"
description: "Write a timestamped archive into .substrate/backups/."
---

```sh
substrate backup
```

Writes a timestamped `.tar.gz` of the substrate into `.substrate/backups/` and prints the
path.

<Note>
  No retention or pruning in v0.7.0 — backups accumulate until you remove them.
</Note>

Take one before anything you are unsure about: a `substrate import --force`, a hand-edit
of several board files, or a template apply into a substrate you care about.

To write the archive somewhere other than `.substrate/backups/`, use
[`substrate export`](/cli/export-import).
